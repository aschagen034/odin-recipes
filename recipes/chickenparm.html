Chicken Parmesan

Description

Chicken Parmesan is a popular Italian-American staple. It consists of breaded chicken that is usually pan-fried and then baked with rich tomato sauce, Italian cheese, and herbs. The whole recipe should take around 45 minutes to complete, with 15-20 minutes of prep times and 20 minutes of actual cooking. For this specific recipe we are not going to go crazy with the tomato sauce. This is because we want to keep the breading on the chicken crispy and not a soggy mess. You can always serve with some sauce on the side just in case someone wants extra sauce. The recipe serves 4 so scale it accordingly to your needs.

Ingredients

  • 4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
  • salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
