Chocolate Chip Cookies

Description

This recipe is the best chocolate chip cookie recipe you will every try. These cookies are perfectly crispy and chewy, doughy yet fully baked, and perfectly sweet. Everything a chocolate chip cookie should be. Hope you enjoy this simple, straightforward, and amazingly delicious recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup salted butter softened
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cup light brown sugar
  • 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt
  • 2 cups chocolate chips (12oz)

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C). Line three baking sheets with a parchment paper and set aside.
  2. In a medium bowl mix flour, baking soda, baking powder and salt. Set aside.
  3. Cream together butter and sugars until combined.
  4. Beat in eggs and vanilla until light (about 1 minute).
  5. Mix in the dry ingredients until combined.
  6. Add chocolate chips and mix well.
  7. Roll 2-3 tablespoons (depending how large you want your cookies) of dough at a time into balls and place them evenly spaced on your prepared cookie sheets.
  8. Bake in preheated oven for apporximately 8-10 minutes. Take them out when they are just barely starting to turn brown.
  9. Let them sit on the baking pan for 5 mintues before removing to the cooling rack.
